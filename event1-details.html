Fashion Revolution Week

In April 2023, we proudly participated in Fashion Revolution Week, a global movement advocating for transparency and sustainability in the fashion industry. Our team organized a series of impactful events, including educational workshops, panel discussions with industry experts, and a community fashion show featuring upcycled designs.

Through these initiatives, we aimed to raise awareness about the environmental and social impacts of fast fashion, inspiring our community to make more conscious choices. The event was a resounding success, fostering meaningful conversations and empowering individuals to take action towards a more sustainable future in fashion!
