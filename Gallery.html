Fashion Revolution Week — 4 June 2023

A vibrant day dedicated to sustainable practices and creative styling. Our community gathered at AWE Gallery for an immersive itinerary featuring hands-on Basic Embroidery, traditional "Wrapped Up in Sarong" styling workshops, and an exclusive documentary screening spotlighting the people behind our garments. From interactive clothes swaps to collaborative mending spaces guided by DSew Club, this event successfully connected fashion lovers with conscious, impactful solutions.
